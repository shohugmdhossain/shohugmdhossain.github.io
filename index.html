Mission

Transforming remote-sensing data into scientific insight.

This website presents research connecting GeoAI, remote sensing, and planetary science. The central goal is to use intelligent geospatial methods to study landscapes, surface processes, and planetary environments across Earth and Mars.

Current Focus

Building AI workflows for geospatial and planetary discovery.

Current work integrates high-resolution imagery, deep learning, geospatial post-processing, and scalable computing to support landform detection, terrain interpretation, and scientific mapping.

Planetary surface mapping
