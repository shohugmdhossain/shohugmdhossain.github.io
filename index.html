Mission

Transforming remote-sensing data into scientific insight.

This homepage is designed as a visual gateway to research connecting GeoAI, remote sensing, and planetary science. Instead of repeating biography or project details, it introduces the central idea: using intelligent geospatial methods to study landscapes, surface processes, and planetary environments across Earth and Mars.

Current Focus

Building AI workflows for planetary and geospatial discovery.

Current research connects high-resolution orbital imagery, deep learning, geospatial post-processing, and scalable computing to detect landforms, characterize terrain, and support data-driven interpretation of complex surfaces.

Planetary surface mapping

Earth observation
